## Customer Segments

Coffee drinkers.

## Early Adopters

People who brew their coffee at home.

## Problem

People have limited choices for freshly brewed high quality coffee.

## Existing Alternatives

Supermarket coffee.
Dunking Donut slash McDonald's.
Home brewed coffee.

## Unique Value Proposition

A third place between work and home.

## High-Level Concept

McDonald's for coffee.

## Solution

Bring Italian coffeehouse tradition to the US.

## Channels

Retail stores, supermarkets, advertising.

## Revenue Streams

Coffee. Five dollars per cup.
Coffee beans, twelve dollars per bag.

## Cost Structure

people
Retail store costs.

## Key Metrics

Number of cups served.
Number of customers.
Average revenue per customer.

## Unfair Advantage

Community convenience and accessibility.
